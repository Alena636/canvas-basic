How to play

Drive a tank around a small battlefield.

Controls

  • W Drive forward
  • S Reverse
  • AD Turn the hull
  • Mouse Aim the turret
  • Esc Back to menu

Map

  • Brick wall: blocks the tank
  • Concrete: blocks the tank
  • Water: not solid yet
  • Bushes: drive right through

How it works

  • The hull and the turret move separately: the keys steer the hull, the mouse aims the turret.
  • The hull turns in place, so you can spin around without moving.
  • If the next step would hit a wall, the tank just stops.
