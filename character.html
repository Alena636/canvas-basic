How to play

Make a little platformer hero run.

Controls

  • ←→ Run left and right
  • Esc Back to menu

How it works

  • The character is cut out of one sprite sheet with all its poses.
  • While running, the run frames swap every 0.2 s, so the legs move.
  • Running left flips the picture like a mirror.
  • Let go of the keys and the character goes back to its standing pose.
