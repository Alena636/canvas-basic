How to play

Pilot a spaceship through a twinkling starfield.

Controls

  • Mouse Aim the ship
  • W Hold to fire the engine and fly
  • Esc Back to menu

How it works

  • The ship's nose always turns toward your cursor.
  • When the cursor is very close to the ship, it keeps its angle so it doesn't jitter.
  • While W is held, the engine flame flickers and the ship flies where its nose points.
  • There are no edges yet: the ship can fly off-screen, so steer it back with the mouse.
